Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your data stays on your device

Expense Tracker stores the months, transactions, budgets and reminders you enter only on your own phone or browser. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not require an account.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No access to your SMS, contacts, location or photos.

Optional features

  • Backups: backup files are saved or shared only when you choose to. Where you save them (for example Google Drive or iCloud) is under that provider's terms.
  • Google Drive backup: if you switch it on, the app stores one backup file in a private app folder of your own Google Drive. We cannot see it.
  • Import: text and PDFs you import are read on your device and are not uploaded.
  • App lock: your PIN is stored on your device as a salted hash. Fingerprint or Face ID is handled by your device and we never receive biometric data.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's data or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device. Delete any backup files you saved yourself.
