Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your data stays on your device

Expense Tracker stores the months, transactions, budgets, reminders and settings you enter only on your own phone or browser. We do not run a server that receives your records, and we do not require an account.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of your records.
  • No access to your SMS, contacts or location.

Features that use your files

  • Bill scanning: the photo you choose is read on your device. It is not uploaded.
  • PDF and text import: read on your device and not uploaded.
  • Backups: backup files and PDF statements are saved or shared only when you choose to. Where you save them (for example Google Drive or iCloud) is under that provider's terms.
  • Google Drive backup (optional, if enabled): stores one backup file in a private app folder of your own Google Drive. We cannot see it.
  • App lock: your PIN is stored on your device as a salted hash. Fingerprint or Face ID is handled by your device and we never receive biometric data.

Optional AI advisor

The money advisor chat works with built-in notes on your device. If the AI mode is available, the questions you type are sent through our server to an AI provider (Groq) to produce an answer. Only if you switch on "Let the advisor see my summary numbers" are summary totals (income, spending, category totals, budgets and EMI total) also sent. Transaction names and notes are never sent. Please do not type account numbers, passwords or other private details into the chat. Groq processes the text under its own privacy policy. The advisor gives general education only and is not financial advice.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's data or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device. Delete any backup files you saved yourself.
