Everything you need, nothing you don't

The website runs the full app, with every feature below.

📅

A page for every month

Create a month, add credits and expenses, and your leftover balance carries forward by itself.

💼

Any source of income

Salary, freelance, business, gifts, or type your own, like pocket money or a scholarship.

📷

Scan a bill

Take a photo of a bill and it becomes an expense. The reading happens on your device.

🧾

Bank-statement PDFs

Download one month or all months, with opening and closing balance and total credit and debit.

📊

Power BI-style dashboard

Click any chart to filter the rest. Trends, category share, a heatmap and legends on every chart.

🎯

Budget planner and alerts

Tell it your income and get a suggested budget, or set every limit yourself. Alerts at 80% and when you go over.

💹

Salary and investment planner

Enter your salary and see how much could go to needs, savings, investments and goals. Change any percentage.

🧠

Smart, private insights

Learns your categories, forecasts the month, flags unusual spending and finds repeating payments.

💬

AI money advisor

Ask about budgeting, SIPs, loans, inflation or the economy, or about your own spending. Built-in notes work offline, and AI can be switched on. General education, not a licensed adviser.

🔔

EMI and SIP reminders

See what is due soon, mark it paid in one tap, or add it to your phone calendar.

⇪

Import from PDF or notes

Paste notes or choose a bank or UPI PDF. Check the rows, then add them.

🔒

PIN and fingerprint lock

Keep your money private behind a PIN, with fingerprint or Face ID where your device allows it.

🌐

English, हिन्दी, मराठी

Switch the app to Hindi or Marathi from Settings.

📴

Works offline

Your data stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.

☁️

Backup and cloud

Back up to a file, share it to Drive or iCloud, or turn on optional Google Drive backup.

Three steps to a clear picture of your money.

Create a monthStart with this month. Next month picks up your leftover balance by itself.
Add, scan or importTap +, scan a bill, or import a PDF or notes. Pick expense or credit and a category.
Plan, see and shareMake a budget, open the dashboard and download a statement PDF whenever you need one.
